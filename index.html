Common questions

Questions small nonprofit teams often ask.

Do we need a new website?

Not necessarily. The workflow is designed to work with an existing website when its structure can support your selected publishing needs. If your website needs broader design or structural improvements, website design or redesign can be scoped separately.

How long does setup take?

Most setups are completed within 14–21 days. The process includes an initial call, system configuration, light website adjustments, and a training session. Your team will be ready to start publishing by the end of setup.

What types of updates can our team publish?

Your setup includes the types of updates your team posts most often — events, donation updates, volunteer opportunities, announcements, program updates, or impact stories. Starter Setup includes 2 types; Expanded Setup includes up to 3. Your team provides the information — FideSpark structures and formats it.

What website platforms do you work with?

We work with Squarespace, Wix, WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, and Framer. Some publishing workflows may require a platform plan that supports third-party integrations — we will confirm this during your initial call. Third-party API costs are not included in the setup or monthly fee.

Why is monthly support required?

The publishing workflow runs on FideSpark's platform. Monthly support keeps your workflow available and supported, and includes a monthly website review with practical recommendations. Without an active monthly plan, the workflow will no longer function — though your existing website content stays in place.

What happens if we stop the monthly plan?

If you cancel, your configured publishing workflow will no longer be available. Your website content remains on your website — only the workflow powered by FideSpark's system stops functioning. We recommend discussing any changes with us before cancelling so we can help you plan accordingly.

Can we get a refund on the setup fee?

Setup fees are non-refundable once payment is received, as work begins immediately. We recommend booking a free call before purchasing to make sure FideSpark is the right fit for your organization.
