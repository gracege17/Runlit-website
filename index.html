FAQ

Questions we hear a lot.

What is Runlit?

Runlit by Fidespark is an AI-powered website and operations system built for 1–5 person small business teams in the US. It lets your team manage your website, bookings, events, and daily content by sending a plain-text message — no developer, no backend, no waiting.

What is the AI assistant exactly?

Custom-built tool connected to your website. Send a message like "add a new event this Saturday at 2pm" and your website updates automatically. Tailored to your business, not a generic chatbot.

I don't have a website yet. Can you help?

Yes. Many clients start from scratch. We handle everything — design, content structure, AI assistant — all in the setup fee.

What does AEO mean?

AI Engine Optimization. Like SEO but for AI tools (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI). We structure your content so these tools find and recommend your business. Included in the Growth plan.

Are there any extra costs I should know about?

Your website may run on platforms like Wix or Shopify, which have their own fees. Third-party tools like Airtable are separate too. We list everything before starting — no surprises.

What if I already have a CRM or other tools?

We can work with existing tools. If upgrades are needed, we'll tell you upfront so you can decide.

What if I need new features later?

New features are quoted separately. Your monthly fee covers maintenance and support for your existing system.

Do I need any technical skills?

No. If you can send a text message, you can run your system.

How long does it take to go live?

Most clients are live within 2–3 weeks of the discovery call. The main factor is how quickly we get your content and feedback.

Is there a contract? Can I cancel?

No long-term contract. You can cancel with 30 days' notice. The setup fee covers the design and build work done upfront.
