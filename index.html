FAQ

Questions small nonprofit teams often ask.

Do we need a new website?

Not necessarily. The Website Operations System is designed to work with an existing website when its current structure can support your selected publishing needs. If your website needs broader design or structural improvements, website design or redesign can be scoped separately.

What can our team publish?

Your setup includes selected routine content types, such as events, donation updates, volunteer opportunities, announcements, program updates, or impact stories. Starter Setup includes 2 configured publishing types, and Expanded Setup includes up to 3.

Is website design included in the setup fee?

Setup includes light adjustments within existing website sections when needed to support your configured publishing workflows. New website design or broader redesign work is a separate service.

Why is monthly support required?

The Website Operations System is an ongoing content operations service. Monthly support keeps your configured publishing workflows available, provides basic usage support, and includes a monthly website review with practical recommendations.

Do you create marketing content for us?

No. Your team provides the information and materials you want to publish. We help structure the publishing workflow and make routine website content easier to manage.

Are analytics included?

Insights Support includes data-informed recommendations when analytics data is already available. GA4, Google Search Console, and key event tracking configuration are available as an optional one-time add-on when needed.

Do your design services include accessibility?

Website design and redesign services include a basic accessibility pass within the agreed design scope. This does not include a formal accessibility audit, compliance review, WCAG or ADA certification, or legal compliance guarantee.

Not sure what your website needs next?

Tell us what your team needs to keep current. We will help identify whether content operations, design support, or both are the right fit.
