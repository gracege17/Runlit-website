Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2, 2026

Who we are

FideSpark is a website operations service for small nonprofits, based in San Francisco, California. If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at getianmeizi@gmail.com.

What information we collect

When you book a free call through our website, Calendly collects your name, email address, and any notes you choose to include. We use this information only to schedule and conduct the call.

We do not operate contact forms, email subscription lists, or any other data collection on this website. We do not use Google Analytics or any other analytics tracking tools. This website does load fonts from Google Fonts, which may log your IP address as part of that request — see the Google Fonts section below.

How we use your information

We use the information collected through Calendly solely to respond to your inquiry and schedule your consultation. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-party services

Google Fonts

This website loads fonts from Google's servers. When your browser requests a font file, Google may log your IP address as part of that request. We use Google Fonts solely to display typefaces and not for analytics or tracking purposes. Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

Calendly

Appointment scheduling is handled by Calendly. When you book a call, your information is subject to Calendly's own privacy policy at calendly.com/privacy.

Anthropic (Claude API)

FideSpark uses Claude, an AI service provided by Anthropic, as part of the publishing system we configure for clients. Content submitted through the publishing system may be processed by Anthropic's API. We do not submit personal information through this system unless required by a specific client service arrangement, in which case it will be disclosed separately. Anthropic's privacy policy is available at anthropic.com/privacy.

Client website platforms

Depending on your organization's website platform (such as Squarespace, Wix, WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, or Framer), your content may also be subject to that platform's own terms and privacy policy.

Data retention

We retain booking information for as long as necessary to manage our client relationships. You may request deletion of your information at any time by contacting us at getianmeizi@gmail.com.

Your rights under California law (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect about you
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt out of the sale of your personal information (we do not sell personal information)
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising these rights

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at getianmeizi@gmail.com. We will respond within 45 days.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent update. Continued use of our website after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
